🛡️ deScroll Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

At deScroll, your privacy is our top priority. deScroll helps you reduce screentime, combat doomscrolling, and improve focus with a productivity launcher. 🌟 Core features such as blocking, usage tracking, scroll counting, and launcher settings run on your device. Some optional features (AI Helper, Support, subscriptions, weather, crash reporting) need to send limited data off your device. We do not collect names, email addresses, or Google accounts. This policy explains exactly what is processed, where, and for how long.

1. Information Processed on Your Device 📊

The following data is processed and stored locally on your device. It is not sent to us, except where you use a feature described in section 2:

  • 📱 App Usage Data (Usage Access, Query All Packages)
    With your consent, deScroll reads app usage statistics (e.g., time spent on apps, short-video scrolls) and your list of installed apps to power insights, blocking, and launcher features. This data is stored in a local database on your device.
  • 🚫 App Blocking & Usage Limits (Accessibility Service)
    deScroll uses Android Accessibility Service to detect the app in the foreground, supported short-video screens, and scroll events, so it can enforce the rules you set in App Shield, Usage Guardian, and Focus Enhancer and count scrolls. This activity stays on your device.
  • 🔔 Notifications (Post Notifications, Notification Control)
    deScroll sends you alerts and, if you enable Notification Control, filters or silences selected app notifications and keeps a notification history on your device.
  • 📲 Overlays & Background Services (Display Over Apps, Foreground Service, Boot Completed)
    Used for floating timers, countdowns, and focus controls, and to restart protection after a reboot. They are not used to collect unrelated activity.
  • 💬 AI Helper Chat History
    Your AI Helper conversations are saved in the app on your device so you can scroll back through them.

2. Information Processed on Our Servers ☁️

Our backend runs on Google Cloud. It only receives data when you use one of these features:

  • 🆔 Anonymous Device Identifier
    A random anonymous ID (Firebase UID) is created for your installation. We store it with your app ID and when it was created and last used, so we can connect you to the backend, enforce daily message limits, and link premium access. It is never linked to your name or email.
  • 🤖 AI Helper
    When you send a message, deScroll sends your message and your recent conversation to our backend, which uses up to the last 10 messages. If the answer needs your deScroll data, the app may also send a limited summary relevant to your question, such as app names, usage time, scroll counts, and your App Shield, Notification Control, or Focus Enhancer settings. Messages are automatically checked for safety, then sent to Google Gemini through Google Cloud Vertex AI to generate the reply. We do not store your AI Helper messages or replies on our servers after the reply is returned. Google processes this data under its Google Cloud terms.
  • 🔢 Daily Message Counter
    We keep a count of how many AI Helper messages you sent today to apply your plan's daily limit. It resets automatically every day.
  • 🎫 Support Tickets
    When you contact support in the app, we store your ticket subject, messages, our replies, ticket status, priority, and app version so we can help you.
  • 💳 Subscriptions
    When you buy or restore premium, we verify your purchase with Google Play and store the details needed to manage access: purchase token, product and plan ID, order ID, subscription status, and expiry date. Payment card details are handled by Google Play and never reach us.
  • 🧾 Technical Logs
    Our servers record basic request logs (time, endpoint, status, response time, and your anonymous ID) and subscription events to keep the service running and investigate problems. These logs do not contain your AI Helper messages.
  • 🌤️ Weather (optional)
    If you enable weather and grant approximate location, your coarse latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo to fetch the temperature. Location is stored only on your device and is never used for advertising.
  • 📈 Crash Reports & Analytics
    Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics may collect crash logs, diagnostics, and high-level feature usage to help us fix bugs and improve reliability.

3. How Long We Keep Data ⏳

  • AI Helper messages: not stored on our servers; processed only to generate the reply.
  • Daily message counter: deleted automatically at the end of each day.
  • Technical logs: kept by Google Cloud Logging for up to 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Subscription records: kept while your subscription is relevant and as needed for billing, fraud prevention, and legal requirements.
  • Support tickets and your anonymous ID record: kept while needed to provide the service, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • On-device data: stays until you clear app data or uninstall deScroll.

4. How Data Is Used 🔧

  • To Reduce Screentime: Usage data powers "Advanced Insights" and "Usage Guardian" on your device.
  • To Block Apps and Protect Focus: App Shield, Focus Enhancer, and Notification Control apply your rules on your device.
  • To Answer AI Helper Questions: Your message and any summary you allow are used only to generate the reply.
  • To Provide Support and Premium Access: Tickets, purchase verification, and daily limits.
  • To Keep deScroll Reliable and Secure: Logs, crash reports, and abuse prevention.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

5. Sharing and Service Providers 🤝

  • Google Cloud (hosting, database, logs) and Google Gemini via Vertex AI (AI Helper replies).
  • Google Firebase (anonymous sign-in, crash reporting, analytics).
  • Google Play Billing (subscriptions).
  • Upstash (stores the daily message counter).
  • Open-Meteo (weather, only if enabled).
  • Authorities, only where required by law.

These providers may process data in countries other than your own, including outside India, under their own safeguards.

6. Data Security 🔒

  • Encryption in Transit: All data sent to our servers uses HTTPS.
  • Anonymous by Design: We do not ask for or store your email, name, or Google account.
  • Minimal Storage: AI Helper conversations are not kept on our servers.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. Your Rights & Choices ⚖️

  • Permissions: Review or revoke any permission in Android settings. Related features may stop working until access is restored.
  • On-Device Data: Delete it by clearing deScroll's app data or uninstalling the app.
  • Server Data: To access or delete data linked to your anonymous ID, email us with the anonymous device ID shown in the app settings. Because we do not collect emails, we cannot find your data by email address. Some billing or legal records may be kept where required.
  • Subscriptions: Manage or cancel them in Google Play Subscriptions.

8. Children’s Privacy 🚸

deScroll is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy 📝

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date, and significant updates will be communicated via the app.
